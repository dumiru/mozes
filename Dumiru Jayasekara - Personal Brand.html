About me

I am a young entrepreneur from Sri Lanka. I have started and helped build several businesses, and I am a partner in others.

My work sits where media, marketing and technology meet. I care about clear products, honest communication and businesses that last.

As an interactive product designer, I design the screens, flows and experiences people use every day, from first idea to final build.

Outside work, I write, share what I learn and talk with founders who are building something of their own.
