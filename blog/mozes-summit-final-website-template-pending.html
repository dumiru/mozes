Common questions

Before you apply

Is this open to the public?+

Seats are application-based rather than first-come, first-served. This keeps the room focused on founders, operators and investors who get real value out of being there.

What's the dress code?+

Smart business casual for both days, with a slightly more relaxed dress code for the evening receptions.

Will sessions be recorded?+

Main stage sessions will be recorded for attendees only. Deal room conversations and workshops stay off the record.

Can I get a refund if I can't attend?+

Passes are refundable up to 30 days before the summit, and always transferable to a colleague at no extra cost.

Do you offer group rates?+

Yes — teams of three or more applying together receive a reduced rate on Delegate and Business passes. Mention your team size in your application.
