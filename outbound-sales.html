Common questions

Before you reach out

How many channels do you use?+

We typically recommend starting with 2-3 key channels where your buyers are most responsive. As you grow, we can expand to additional channels based on performance and goals.

What types of outreach do you run?+

We run a mix of cold email, LinkedIn messaging, cold calling, video outreach, SMS follow-ups, and account-based touchpoints - all tailored to your brand voice and target buyer preferences.

How involved do I need to be?+

We handle everything and send you a monthly outreach plan for approval, and we'll loop you in on strategic decisions, but we keep the process simple and hands-off for you.

How is performance measured?+

Beyond vanity metrics (emails sent, calls made), we track replies, meetings booked, pipeline, and ROI. We provide monthly reports that connect outreach activity to actual business outcomes.

What happens if I need to pause or cancel?+

We ask for a 30-day initial commitment to build momentum. After that, you can pause or cancel with 30 days' notice — no hidden fees or penalties.
