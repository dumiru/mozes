Common questions

Before you reach out

How many platforms do you manage?+

We typically recommend starting with 2-3 key platforms where your audience is most active. As you grow, we can expand to additional platforms based on performance and goals.

What types of content do you create?+

We create a mix of static posts, carousels, stories, short-form video (Reels, TikTok), long-form video (YouTube), and platform-specific content - all tailored to your brand voice and audience preferences.

How involved do I need to be?+

We handle everything and send you a monthly content calendar for approval, and we'll loop you in on strategic decisions, but we keep the process simple and hands-off for you.

How is performance measured?+

Beyond vanity metrics (likes, follows), we track engagement rate, reach, clicks, conversions, sentiment, and ROI. We provide monthly reports that connect social activity to actual business outcomes.

What happens if I need to pause or cancel?+

We ask for a 30-day initial commitment to build momentum. After that, you can pause or cancel with 30 days' notice — no hidden fees or penalties.
